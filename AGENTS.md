"Poster" Pebble Watchface

- Designed for the Pebble Time 2 (200x228)

## Layout

- `src/c/main.c`: the watchface; everything is drawn in one layer's update proc
- `src/pkjs/index.js`: weather fetch on the phone and the settings page
- `src/pkjs/config.js`: the settings page layout
- `resources/images/`: pre-rendered 1-bit digit and rotated rail glyphs (the watch can't rotate text, and 110 px glyphs exceed the font glyph-size limit). The digits are tinted at draw time through their palette.
- `tools/setup.sh`: downloads the fonts and runs `tools/gen_assets.py`, which renders the bitmaps. Both are gitignored, as binaries.
- `src/c/assets.h`: committed, but regenerated by the same script

To change the digit font, size or the rail font, edit `tools/gen_assets.py` and rerun `sh tools/setup.sh`. To change the accent, edit `ACCENT` in `main.c`.

## Fonts

Chivo Mono (Omnibus-Type) and Silkscreen (Jason Kottke), both under the SIL Open Font License. `setup.sh` fetches them and their licence texts from google/fonts.
